Our Story

Established with a vision for precision healthcare.

Established with a vision to support scientific discovery and precision healthcare, our lab combines cutting-edge technology with expert knowledge to deliver accurate, reliable, and timely results.

At BJ Micro Lab, we specialize in a wide range of testing services, including antimicrobial susceptibility testing, genetic analysis, molecular diagnostics (PCR, RT-PCR, sequencing), fungal identification, biochemical testing, and much more. Our services are designed to meet the growing needs of academic research, clinical studies, pharmaceutical development, and industrial quality assurance.

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality, transparency, and scientific integrity. Every test we perform is backed by rigorous protocols and handled by qualified professionals with a passion for innovation and accuracy.

We work closely with universities, hospitals, biotech firms, and individual researchers to provide tailored solutions that meet precise scientific goals — and we're constantly evolving, adopting global standards and updating our equipment and methodologies to stay at the forefront of diagnostic science.
