Sequencing Services

Sequencing is the final step in many genetic workflows — used for confirming gene edits, identifying mutations, or characterizing unknown DNA. We offer Sanger sequencing with high accuracy, fast delivery, and expert support for diverse research and diagnostic needs.

How it works

Sanger sequencing remains the gold standard for short-read, high-fidelity DNA analysis. We accept both PCR products and plasmid DNA, performing purification and cycle sequencing using fluorescent-labeled dideoxynucleotides. Capillary electrophoresis then generates clear, easy-to-interpret chromatograms up to 1000 base pairs.

Our service supports custom or standard primers — and if you're unsure which to use, we can assist with primer design to ensure full coverage of your gene or insert. All sequencing data is delivered with chromatogram files (.ab1), base-call text files, and quality score summaries.

Where this service is used

  • Clone validation
  • Mutation detection
  • Phylogenetic analysis and barcoding applications
  • Publication-ready QA for synthetic biology, microbiology, and plant sciences
