SVC-04 · Nucleic Acid Extraction

DNA / RNA Extraction

Reliable molecular analysis begins with high-quality nucleic acids. Our extraction service delivers clean, intact DNA and RNA that holds up through every downstream step.

How it works

We extract DNA or RNA from a wide range of sample types — blood, tissue, cultures, swabs, and plant material — using protocols selected to protect yield and purity. Each extraction is checked for concentration and quality before it's released for downstream use.

This service is most often the first step in a larger workflow: samples extracted here typically move on to PCR, sequencing, or genetic analysis, so we hold extraction quality to the standard those next steps need.

Where this service is used

  • Preparing samples for PCR or sequencing
  • Research requiring high-purity nucleic acid
  • Clinical and diagnostic sample prep
  • Plant and environmental sample studies
