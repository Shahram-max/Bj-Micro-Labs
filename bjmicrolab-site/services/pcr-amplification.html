PCR & Amplification

Our PCR & Amplification services offer high-sensitivity nucleic acid detection — including conventional PCR and RT-PCR — for fast, dependable results across research and diagnostic applications.

How it works

Target nucleic acid is amplified using validated primer sets and thermal cycling protocols, with results confirmed through gel electrophoresis or real-time detection depending on the assay. RT-PCR is available for RNA targets, including viral and gene-expression work.

We support both standard assays and custom primer sets — and if you're not sure which approach fits your target, our team can help you scope the right protocol before you send samples.

Where this service is used

  • Pathogen and viral detection (RT-PCR)
  • Gene expression studies
  • Genotyping and target confirmation
  • Pre-sequencing amplification
