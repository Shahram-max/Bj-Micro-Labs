Cell Culture

BJ Micro Lab offers professional cell culture services, enabling researchers to grow, maintain, and study live cells under controlled laboratory conditions — for basic biological research, drug development, or toxicity screening.

How it works

We specialize in mammalian and microbial cell lines, supporting short-term cultures or long-term propagation. Our facilities include biosafety cabinets, CO₂ incubators, and temperature-controlled environments to maintain optimal conditions for cell health and proliferation.

We also support custom cell culture requests — such as pre-treatment with drugs, co-culture conditions, or seeding cells on custom scaffolds or substrates for tissue engineering. Every project follows rigorous SOPs to prevent contamination and maintain cell line authenticity, which is crucial for reproducible results.

Where this service is used

  • Basic biological research
  • Drug development and toxicity screening
  • Co-culture and tissue-engineering setups
  • Long-term propagation and colony maintenance
