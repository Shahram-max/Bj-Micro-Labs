SVC-02 · Microbial Testing

Microbial Testing

BJ Micro Lab's Microbial Testing service supports hospitals, researchers, and institutions with identification, susceptibility, and quality-assurance testing they can act on quickly.

How it works

Samples are cultured and identified using standard microbiological methods, with antimicrobial susceptibility testing performed where relevant to guide treatment or research decisions. Fungal identification and biochemical testing are also available alongside standard bacterial workups.

Every result goes through internal review before it's released, so clinicians and researchers can rely on the report without needing to re-verify it themselves.

Where this service is used

  • Hospital and clinical diagnostics
  • Antimicrobial susceptibility testing
  • Fungal identification and biochemical testing
  • Industrial and environmental quality assurance
