What We Do

A lab built around accuracy, transparency, and people who actually explain things.

At BJ Micro Lab, we specialize in antimicrobial susceptibility testing, genetic analysis, molecular diagnostics — PCR, RT-PCR, and sequencing — fungal identification, biochemical testing, and more, serving academic research, clinical studies, pharmaceutical development, and industrial quality assurance.
